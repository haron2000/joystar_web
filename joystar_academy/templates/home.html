Help & Info

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school operating hours?

Our main school gate opens at 7:00 AM. Classes run from 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM, followed by co-curricular clubs until 4:30 PM.

How do I register for school transport?

Transport application forms are available at the admissions office or through the online portal during enrollment.

Is the CBC curriculum fully integrated?

Yes, we offer full Competency-Based Curriculum learning pathways from Playgroup up to Junior Secondary School (Grade 9).
